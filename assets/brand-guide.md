# MOJEALTEREGO — Brand System / Stage 1

## Master mark
The supplied logo is the primary brand mark. Do not redraw, replace, simplify, recolor, or substitute it with text-only AM in production layouts.

Master components: crown, laurel, AM monogram, ANDRZEJ MIKULSKI wordmark, ornament, handwritten Mojealterego signature.

## Surface
Primary material language: black Alcantara / black aniline-leather visual surface. Use subtle microtexture, low-sheen highlights, restrained grain, and soft directional light. Avoid plastic, glossy glass, or generic flat black gradients.

## Accent palette
- 24K Gold: #D6A94F / highlight #F6D991
- Snow / Ivory: #F5F1E8
- Burgundy: #651D2A
- Royal Blue: #1E3F73
- Bottle Green: #17382D
- Titanium: #8F9398
- Platinum: #D5D4D0
- Copper: #A6653F

## Usage
Gold is the signature accent and remains stable across the brand. Section accents may use Burgundy, Royal Blue, Bottle Green, Titanium, Platinum, or Copper according to domain context. The master logo itself is never recolored per section.

## Lockups
1. Master: supplied full logo.
2. Header: supplied logo in proportional compact presentation.
3. Hero: full supplied logo at large scale on black Alcantara.
4. Mark: AM/crown/laurel where the complete wordmark cannot fit.
5. Favicon/app icon: AM/crown mark only.

## Typography
Primary editorial serif: Cormorant Garamond.
UI / utility sans: Inter.
Signature script: use supplied vector signature as the authority; a script font is only a fallback for contexts where the vector signature cannot be used.

## Prohibited
- generic text-only AM replacing the supplied mark
- stretching the logo
- changing logo proportions
- placing the master logo on noisy or low-contrast backgrounds
- arbitrary gold gradients that overpower the supplied metallic treatment
- using all accent colors simultaneously in one component
