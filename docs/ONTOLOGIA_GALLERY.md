## Source integrity

- The source images are owner-supplied project material.
- Do not redraw, reinterpret, replace or generate substitutes for these images when implementing the gallery.
- Do not invent scientific claims beyond text actually present in the supplied visual material.
- Visible labels and mathematical notation belong to the source artwork and should remain part of the artwork.
- The gallery UI should not add explanatory captions that imply facts not supplied by the owner.
- The two compact landscape variants (#07 and #08) are retained because they were supplied as part of the same visual set; do not discard them as duplicates without an explicit owner decision.
